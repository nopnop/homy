Transform your new tab page into a stylish periodic table for your most-used bookmarks.

## Features

✨ **Clean Interface**: Replace Chrome's default new tab with a clean, customizable bookmark grid
🎨 **Customization**: Choose colors with transparency effects to match your style
📌 **Bookmark Management**: Create and edit bookmarks directly from any page using the browser toolbar
🔄 **Drag & Drop Organization**: Arrange your bookmarks exactly how you want them
☁️ **Chrome Sync**: Automatic synchronization across all your devices
📁 **Import/Export**: Back up and restore your bookmarks and settings with JSON files
🔒 **Privacy-First**: No tracking, no analytics - your data stays yours

## Quick Tips

- Use square brackets to customize bookmark display names: `[G+] Google+` shows as "G+"
- Right-click any bookmark to edit or remove it
- Drag bookmarks to reorder them in your grid

## Open Source & Privacy

Homy is completely open source (MIT License) and privacy-focused:
- View the source code: https://github.com/nopnop/homy
- No data collection or tracking
- All bookmark data stays on your device and Chrome sync
